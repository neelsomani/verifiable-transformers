## Exact folded implementation

For each fixed input, the verifier will convert every selected FP32 constant
to its exact binary rational, execute the frozen program with exact semantics,
and externally certify every LeakyReLU sign used by `mlp_0`. The certified
region is compiled to an affine residual map; selected calibrated `W_V`, `b_V`,
and bias-free `W_O` slices and the exact selected topology are then
constant-folded. Shared rational constants and repeated certified signatures
may be cached.

Before use on `D`, folded and monolithic exact-rational candidate logits must
be equal on all preserved anchor sequences. A disagreement is a technical
failure, not a tolerance event.

Functional equivalence, content invariance, and circuit-internal edge
necessity use ground exact comparisons under the registered repository
semantics. Continuous robustness uses the registered per-input margin LP and
threshold epsilon `0.01`, reporting the certified maximum epsilon per input.
A fixed trace is accepted only when its exact sign certificate covers the
claimed perturbation region. Any crossing region is split; if it cannot be
exhausted, the result is `UNKNOWN`, never an assumed proof.

Every property emits resumable per-input records with input/example hashes,
assertion attribution, encode/fold time, solve time, exact decisions and
margins, witnesses or counterexamples, sign-certificate status, and topology
and constant hashes. Aggregate claims are made only after all 1,280 frozen
rows are present and their order/hashes revalidate.
